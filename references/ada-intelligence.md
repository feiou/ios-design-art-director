## Cross-Year Design Principles

These are inferences from Apple's award descriptions, not formal requirements.

### Focus the Product Around One Meaningful Value

Winning products often make a narrow promise exceptional: gratitude, critical safety information, focused creation, lunar planning, or a small moment of reflection. Feature count is not the standard. Clarity of purpose and depth of execution are.

Apply this by naming the core human value, protecting the primary task from chrome, and removing features that dilute the promise.

### Make Sophistication Feel Approachable

Procreate Dreams, Play, Feather, and Tide Guide expose technically rich capabilities without making users carry the system's complexity. They combine progressive disclosure, direct manipulation, strong defaults, and clear feedback.

Apply this by keeping the first success fast, moving expert power close but not in the way, and letting manipulation teach the model.

### Design for the Real Context, Not an Abstract Screen

Crouton keeps attention on cooking. Moonlitt keeps attention on the sky. Watch Duty prioritizes clarity and reliability during emergencies. Context determines density, glanceability, input, feedback, and interruption behavior.

Apply this by writing the physical and emotional context before laying out controls.

### Build Accessibility Into the Mechanism

oko turns haptics and audio into the core safety experience. Speechify centers alternative perception. Guitar Wiz uses spoken guidance, Dynamic Type, Increased Contrast, and Differentiate Without Color. Accessibility is strongest when it creates or improves the product's main value.

Apply this by designing multimodal information and flexible input from the first concept pass.

### Create a Coherent Sensory World

Rooms, Bears Gratitude, CapWords, grug, Feather, and Tide Guide show that memorability comes from a unified theme across illustration, type, color, motion, sound, and haptics. Cohesion matters more than visual loudness.

Apply this by defining a small set of sensory rules and refusing effects that do not belong to them.

### Use Emotion With Restraint and Specificity

Warmth, reassurance, curiosity, urgency, reflection, and wonder appear in different winners. The emotion fits the product's purpose; it is not a universal layer of whimsy.

Apply this by naming the intended feeling for each key moment and choosing copy, timing, motion, sound, and haptics that support it.

### Use Apple Technology to Change the Experience

Award descriptions reward Apple Pencil, PencilKit, haptics, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Metal, spatial interfaces, Spatial Audio, and Liquid Glass when they make an interaction newly possible, more accessible, or better suited to the platform.

Apply this by asking what user benefit the technology unlocks. Reject technology used only as a demo.

### Preserve Platform Fit While Showing Authorship

Native does not mean visually anonymous. Winners rely on familiar behaviors and platform capabilities while expressing a distinctive voice through content, interaction details, pacing, illustration, and sensory craft.

Apply this by keeping navigation and control behavior legible, then concentrating originality in product-specific moments.

## Interaction Patterns Worth Reusing

- Teach through the first meaningful action instead of explanatory onboarding.
- Keep essential actions discoverable; add gestures as accelerators.
- Use direct manipulation for creative, spatial, and data-rich work.
- Pair state changes with immediate visual, haptic, or audio confirmation.
- Make errors reversible and preserve work across interruption.
- Adapt density and controls to device, input, attention, and physical context.
- Use animation to preserve spatial continuity and explain causality.
- Design permission requests around a visible user benefit and a graceful denied state.

## Anti-Patterns

- Copying the surface appearance of an award winner.
- Treating minimalism as absence of hierarchy or useful context.
- Applying glass, blur, gradients, or oversized type without product reason.
- Hiding essential actions behind undiscoverable gestures.
- Using animation that delays work or ignores Reduce Motion.
- Treating accessibility, empty states, failure, or localization as cleanup.
- Claiming an Apple rule when the conclusion is only an inferred preference.
